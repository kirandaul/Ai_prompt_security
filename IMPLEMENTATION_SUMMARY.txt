  Expected: API_KEY_DETECTOR
  ↓ Can Match ↓
  - SSN_PASSPORT_DETECTOR    (PII with API_KEY_DETECTOR expectation)
  - BANKING_DETECTOR         (COMPLIANCE with API_KEY_DETECTOR expectation)

  - INTERNAL_IP_DETECTOR     (INFRA internal IPs)
  - CLOUD_RESOURCE_DETECTOR  (INFRA cloud resources)
  
This is semantically correct - all these are sensitive data not to be shared.
